Welcome to the **StoreSuite** documentation. StoreSuite is a free, open-source
WordPress plugin that puts your entire WooCommerce store behind one polished
frontend dashboard - with AI product content and image generation built in.

![The StoreSuite frontend dashboard](/docs/images/dashboard-home.jpg)

## What you get

- **[Dashboard](/docs/dashboard)** - KPIs, a net-sales chart, top products,
  recent orders, and quick actions, all on one screen.
- **[Products](/docs/products)** - every product type (simple, variable,
  grouped, external/affiliate), with quick edit, bulk edit, filters, and CSV
  export.
- **[Product CSV import](/docs/product-import)** - a four-step wizard that
  uploads a CSV, maps its columns, and creates or updates products in bulk.
- **[Attributes](/docs/attributes)**, **[categories](/docs/categories)**,
  **[tags](/docs/tags)**, and **[brands](/docs/brands)** - the whole catalog
  taxonomy, nested under Products in the sidebar.
- **[Orders](/docs/orders)** - view, create, and edit orders with refunds, fees,
  shipping lines, notes, and customer history (HPOS compatible).
- **[Coupons](/docs/coupons)** - full WooCommerce coupon management, including
  usage restrictions and limits.
- **[Analytics](/docs/analytics)** - a full report suite with WooCommerce-admin
  parity: Revenue, Orders, Products, Variations, Categories, Coupons, Taxes,
  Downloads, Stock, and Customers.
- **[AI Studio](/docs/ai-studio)** - generate product copy and images using your
  own AI provider.
- **[Notifications](/docs/notifications)** - a bell in the header that badges new
  orders, customer registrations, and product reviews as they happen.
- **[Dark mode](/docs/dark-mode)** - a per-user light/dark toggle covering the
  whole dashboard, with four dark palettes to choose from.
- **[Account](/docs/account)** - profile, addresses, and password, without
  visiting wp-admin.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Version |
| --- | --- |
| WordPress | 6.9 or higher (tested up to 7.1) |
| PHP | 8.1 or higher |
| WooCommerce | Active (HPOS supported) |
| AI features | WordPress 7.0+ with a configured AI provider |
